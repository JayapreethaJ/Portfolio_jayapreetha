About Me

Analytical Engineering Foundation With Digital Design Passion

I am a Full Stack Developer and 3D Designer with a B.Tech degree in Chemical Engineering from Puducherry Technological University (2019–2023, CGPA 8.46). My background bridges engineering analysis, modern web development, UI/UX design, and 3D product visualization using Blender.

Having worked as a Relationship Manager at TenMind Global IT Solutions (March 2026 – October 2026), I coordinated client relationships, outreach, and administrative operations. Previously, as an Assistant Quality & Production Executive at Hindustan Unilever Limited, I applied structured problem-solving and cross-functional quality control to production workflows.

My creative and technical work encompasses building responsive web applications using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, creating intuitive Figma wireframes and interactive prototypes, and modeling realistic 3D product visualizations with dedicated lighting and material texturing.
